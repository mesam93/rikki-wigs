---
name: Order tariff scope
description: Scope of the flat post-tax tariff on wig orders and receipts
---

The flat $25 tariff is added after tax for newly created orders and receipts based on those orders. Older orders and already-issued receipt snapshots retain their original totals; do not silently backfill the charge.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose new orders and receipts only when asked how to treat historical financial records.

**How to apply:** Future edits and newly issued receipts should preserve the tariff status of their underlying order. Any retroactive charge to historical orders requires a separate request.
