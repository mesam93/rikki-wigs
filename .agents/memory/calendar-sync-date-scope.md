name: Calendar sync date scope
description: Owner's chosen boundary for historical appointments and Google Calendar
---

Do not newly add past appointments to Google Calendar just because they remain pending or confirmed. Sync today's and future appointments automatically; retain and update links for events that were created while the appointment was upcoming.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose to leave four past appointments off Google rather than backfill every active-status appointment from the booking database.

**How to apply:** Scope catch-up to today and forward in the business timezone. When an admin edits an older appointment without an event link, do not create a new Google event; do not remove already linked events solely because time has passed.
