---
name: Historical order review
description: Why incomplete or inconsistent historical wig orders stay visible but cannot produce receipts
---

Retain each meaningful historical spreadsheet order even when identity, date, or payment information is missing or the spreadsheet's financial totals disagree with recalculated amounts. Keep the original business-cell values available to the owner. Do not invent a zero payment or missing date, silently discard the row, or issue a receipt until the owner reviews and saves a valid order.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose flagged, editable records rather than skipping incomplete rows. An empty historical payment cell is not evidence of a zero payment, and some spreadsheet balances disagree with current calculations.

**How to apply:** When changing historical-order display, edit validation, financial summaries, migration tools, or receipt behavior, maintain traceability and distinguish unknown values from actual zeroes.
